# Sample Lecture Notes - CPSC 481.07 AI Literacy

## Week 1: Introduction to AI Agents

### Key Concepts
- **Intelligent Agents**: Systems that perceive their environment and take actions to achieve goals
- **Agent Architecture**: Components include sensors, actuators, reasoning, and learning
- **Bounded Rationality**: Agents operate with limited knowledge and computational resources

### Discussion Points
- How do agents differ from traditional programs?
- What are the ethical implications of autonomous agents?
- How can we ensure agent safety and reliability?

### Reading Assignment
- AIMA Chapter 2: Intelligent Agents (focus on agent types and environments)
- Consider: What makes an agent "intelligent"?

## Week 2: Search and Problem Solving

### Key Concepts
- **Problem-Solving Agents**: Goal-directed agents that use search algorithms
- **Search Spaces**: State spaces, actions, and goal states
- **Uninformed vs Informed Search**: BFS, DFS, A* algorithm

### Discussion Points
- When should we use different search strategies?
- How do real-world constraints affect search algorithm choice?
- What are the limitations of classical search approaches?

### Reading Assignment
- AIMA Chapter 3: Solving Problems by Searching
- Consider: How do humans search differently from AI systems?

## Week 3: Knowledge Representation

### Key Concepts
- **Knowledge Bases**: Storing and organizing information for AI systems
- **Logical Reasoning**: Propositional and predicate logic
- **Ontologies**: Structured representations of concepts and relationships

### Discussion Points
- How do we represent uncertainty in knowledge?
- What are the challenges of knowledge acquisition?
- How can we validate knowledge base consistency?

### Reading Assignment
- AIMA Chapter 7: Logical Agents
- Consider: What knowledge do you need to represent for different AI applications?

## Week 4: Machine Learning Fundamentals

### Key Concepts
- **Learning from Data**: Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning
- **Training vs Testing**: Overfitting, generalization, and validation
- **Feature Engineering**: Selecting and transforming input data

### Discussion Points
- What are the ethical concerns with machine learning?
- How do we ensure ML systems are fair and unbiased?
- What happens when ML systems fail?

### Reading Assignment
- AIMA Chapter 18: Learning from Examples
- Consider: How can we make ML systems more interpretable?
